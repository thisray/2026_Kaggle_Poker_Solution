# Submitted-evidence case reviews

The following examples were selected from the original `r10_ci.csv` submission. The behavior names are model predictions. These short descriptions support review of particular hands; they do not establish coordination or player intent. Because the retrieval system chose these hands, their frequency is not an estimate of how the pair behaved across all shared play. Amounts are in big blinds (BB).

## 1. Repeated large losses — directed transfer

**Pair:** `P8FA9939F1886`<br>
**Predicted behavior:** `directed_transfer`<br>
**Submitted evidence:** `HDBA527F2C2D221`, `HA08CD42774F1F5`, `HD9EB30F28B5458`, `HE8DFAF2396BF2B`, `H950EF8646547F1`

**Observable behavior.** The same member lost to the other member across the five retrieved hands. With 8-3 suited, the player called a flop bet and a large turn raise, then moved all-in on the river; the partner's pocket sevens won a 301.5-BB pot. In another hand, 4-3 offsuit committed 54.5 BB preflop before folding to the partner's flop shove. The combination of weak starting holdings, substantial contributions, and repeated losses in one direction motivates the transfer interpretation.

**Plausible benign alternative.** Loose calling, unsuccessful aggression, and deep-stack variance against a stronger opponent can generate similar hands. Comparison with this player's decisions against other opponents would be needed to assess partner specificity.
